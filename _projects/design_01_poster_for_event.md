This project page highlights scientific seminars, academic outreach activities, and promotional materials designed for Korean scientific communities in Belgium. These activities aimed to facilitate interdisciplinary exchange between researchers, engineers, students, and industry professionals working across life sciences, engineering, agriculture, quantum technologies, and computational sciences.

My contributions included event planning, visual communication, poster design, programme structuring, and digital promotion for scientific seminars and community events organised in collaboration with academic institutions and scientific associations.

## Selected events

### Spring Scientific Symposium and Science & Humanities Seminar (2024)

Jointly organised by KOSEA Belgium and the Korea-Europe Quantum Science and Technology Cooperation Center (KE-QSTCC), this event focused on emerging developments in quantum science, quantum technology policy, drug delivery systems, and interdisciplinary scientific communication.
